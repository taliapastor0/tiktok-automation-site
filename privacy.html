Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

1. Scope

TikTok Automation is a private, owner-operated integration between TikTok, Composio, and AI-assisted workflows. It is not intended for external users.

2. Information accessed

Subject to permissions actually granted, the integration may access TikTok account identifiers, basic profile information, authorization tokens, and content or publishing-related data. Additional categories must be disclosed here before any corresponding feature is enabled.

3. Purposes

Data is used to authenticate the owner's TikTok account, run explicitly configured workflows, prepare or submit content when authorized, and diagnose integration errors.

4. Service providers and transfers

TikTok and Composio process information as part of the connection. If an AI model, automation host, or storage provider receives account or content data, that provider and the relevant transfer must be documented before enabling the workflow. Their privacy terms may also apply.

5. Storage and security

OAuth credentials or tokens may be stored or processed by integration providers. The owner should restrict access, protect secrets, and review provider retention and security settings. This policy does not claim that no data is stored.

6. Retention and deletion

The owner may disconnect the TikTok integration and revoke authorization. Revocation may not automatically erase information already retained by third-party providers; deletion must also be requested or performed through their available controls. Specific retention periods should be added once the deployment configuration is confirmed.

7. Rights and inquiries

The owner can review authorized permissions, revoke access, and request deletion from relevant providers as applicable. For privacy inquiries, use the contact information below.

8. Updates

This policy should be revised whenever permissions, data flows, processors, or retention practices change.
